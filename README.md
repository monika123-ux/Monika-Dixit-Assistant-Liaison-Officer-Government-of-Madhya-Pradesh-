# Monika Dixit

Assistant Liaison Officer, Government of Madhya Pradesh — working toward a PhD in AI governance and public administration.

## About

I work at the intersection of public administration and technology. Fifteen years split between technical education and state government service: eight years coordinating between Madhya Pradesh and the central government at Madhya Pradesh Bhawan, New Delhi, and seven years before that training Computer Operator and Programming Assistant (COPA) students at a Government ITI.

I'm currently applying to PhD programs, aiming to study — full-time — a question my own job raises every day: how do civil servants actually adopt AI tools without any official framework behind them, and what oversight does that need?

## Research

My work looks at generative AI adoption inside government administrative work — specifically how Indian state government employees use AI informally, without official deployment or governance frameworks. The core questions are trust, accountability, and human oversight, grounded in my own experience with e-Office and scholarship portal systems.

**Working paper:** *Human-AI Collaboration in State E-Governance Systems: A Research-in-Progress Study of Usability, Trust, and Adoption in e-Office and Scholarship Portals* — SSRN, pilot data from 11 respondents, applying the UTAUT framework.
